## What is RemoteStar?

RemoteStar is an AI hiring platform that replaces resume screening with verified skill interviews. Candidates take a single AI video interview, get a scored skill profile, and apply to jobs with proof instead of a PDF. Recruiters get pre-vetted shortlists in hours, not weeks.

70% of resumes get ignored because they all look the same. We fix that.

## For candidates

Skip the screening. Get hired faster.

- **AI video interview** with role-specific, dynamic questions
- **Instant scored report** with skill breakdown and feedback
- **Verified profile link** to attach to any application
- **Retake with feedback** so a bad first answer is not the end
- **Job matching** against 1,000+ roles scanned daily

Average candidate goes from interview to matched offers in hours, not the usual weeks of silence.

## For recruiters

Stop reading 500 resumes to find 5 good ones.

- **Pre-screened, skill-verified candidates** ranked by fit score
- **Bias-free evaluation** based on what people can actually do
- **Continuous sourcing** across 1,000+ roles scanned every day
- **Faster time-to-hire** by skipping the first two interview rounds

## How it works

1. Candidate takes the AI video interview
2. AI scores skills and generates a verified profile
3. Profile link goes on the CV, LinkedIn, and job applications
4. Matched roles surface automatically; recruiters reach out
